Only Sprint 1 gets a day-by-day plan right now.
At the end of Sprint 1, we use what you actually learned to define Sprint 2. No speculative planning for work that will change.

Time constraint

4 hours is the committed weekly core. 6 hours is the ceiling. The plan is designed to fit around your existing leadership role. The highest-value work wins.
