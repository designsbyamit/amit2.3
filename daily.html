01 · Sprint system

Six sprints. Six exit conditions.

Each sprint ends with something tangible. Preparation never becomes an excuse to delay the market.

Sprint 1 · Sep 26 - Oct 10

POSITION + PRIME

Primary exit: Everything about positioning, networking and priming is complete.

Primary pathPositioning, target roles, CV, LinkedIn, portfolio, 5 proof cases, target companies, network map, recruiter map, career stories, first conversations, video practice.
Fallback pathA durable Executive Career Profile that remains valuable even if you stay where you are.
Position, profile, proof and network are ready. The market can understand who you are.
Sprint 2 · Oct 11 - Oct 25

ENTER THE MARKET

Primary exit: Applications, referrals and recruiter/hiring-leader outreach are fully active.

Primary path15-20 targeted applications, 20+ outreach messages, 5+ referral conversations, recruiter conversations, active opportunity tracker, targeted learning for identified gaps.
Fallback pathA meaningful professional market around you: warm relationships, visible expertise and a repeatable outreach engine.
You are officially competing in the market, not preparing to compete.
Sprint 3 · Oct 26 - Nov 9

INTERVIEW + COMMUNICATION

Primary exit: You can articulate your value clearly and confidently.

Primary pathLeadership story bank, product/business stories, AI stories, 15+ recorded answers, 3 short videos/week, 1 longer video/week, mock interviews.
Fallback pathA stronger executive communicator and a visible body of thinking, even independent of the job search.
You can explain your experience as leadership impact, not a history of projects.
Sprint 4 · Nov 10 - Nov 24

INTERVIEW + VISIBILITY

Primary exit: Interviews and external visibility become the main engine.

Primary path3+ recruiter/hiring conversations weekly where possible, active interviews, 5 networking conversations/week, 2 short videos/week, 1 strong POV/week.
Fallback pathA stronger market presence that can continue generating opportunities beyond individual applications.
The pipeline is strengthening and you know where the market is responding.
Sprint 5 · Nov 25 - Dec 9

CONVERT

Primary exit: Serious opportunities move toward final rounds and offers.

Primary pathOpportunity-specific preparation, company/problem research, 30/60/90 thinking, executive conversations, negotiation preparation.
Fallback pathClear evidence about where the market is resisting you and what to change next.
You are converting opportunities rather than simply collecting interviews.
Sprint 6 · Dec 10 - Dec 24

CLOSE + TRANSITION

Primary exit: Offer accepted and joining plan locked, or fallback path activated.

Primary pathFinal rounds, offer evaluation, negotiation, resignation/transition planning, joining plan.
Fallback pathA deliberate next move even if timing slips: stronger market position, active pipeline, capability plan and multiple viable paths.
You finish December with either a new firm or significantly stronger career optionality.
THIS WEEK · Sep 26 - Oct 2 · 4-6 hours

Prime the move. Do not build everything.

Week outcome: leave this week with a sharp leadership position, the first version of your story, your first target list and your first market conversations.

Core 4 hours1h positioning: finalise target role + one-line positioning. 1h proof: choose and outline 5 executive cases. 1h network: map 15 people and send 5 warm messages. 1h articulation: record your 2-minute career story twice and review it.
Optional 1-2 hoursSet up the target-company list and recruiter list, or record one 3-5 minute video on your design/AI POV. Do not spend this time polishing the website.

Every week: 4-hour minimum, 6-hour ceiling

Protect the ceiling. This is a career transition alongside a demanding role, not a second full-time job.

Do less. Make every hour compound.
The website is not a weekly deliverable. It is infrastructure. Your scarce hours go to market movement, proof and communication.

Weekly operating rhythm

Mon: market + applications
Tue: proof + case work
Wed: video + articulation
Thu: networking + interviews
Fri: POV + market review
Sat: course/workshop/deep learning if a real gap exists
Sun: review + next sprint/week planning

Non-negotiable daily rule

1 market move + 1 proof move + 1 communication move.

Learning is added only when evidence from the market shows a capability gap. No certificate collecting.
