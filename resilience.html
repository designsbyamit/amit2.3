06 · Resilience

Make the plan work even when the market doesn't.

Hiring freezes, delayed decisions, internal approvals, competing candidates and timing are outside your control. The system therefore measures progress beyond the offer.

Three definitions of success

Best caseI have accepted the right offer and have a joining plan.
Strong fallbackI have a stronger market position, active relationships, visible expertise, better executive communication and multiple viable paths.

Plan B ladder

1. Ideal: Director / Head-level product design role
2. Adjacent: Design + AI / product design / transformation leadership
3. Strategic: Design strategy, advisory or fractional leadership
4. Independent: Consulting + workshops + speaking + advisory
5. Internal leverage: Expand current scope or negotiate a strategic mandate

Diagnose before changing course

No responses: positioning / CV / targeting
Conversations, no interviews: profile-market fit
Interviews, no second round: communication / case articulation
Final rounds, no offer: executive presence / strategic depth / role fit
Offers, hesitation: role / company / compensation evaluation
