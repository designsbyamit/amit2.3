02 / Position

The market should understand why your combination of experience matters now.

A design leader who connects product, business, systems and AI to build better experiences and stronger design organisations.
Working hypothesis. Pressure-test it through conversations and interviews.

Working thesis

I turn complexity into clarity, and clarity into capability.

Your distinctive intersection

Human-centred + systems-minded + business-aware + future-facing.

Fastest gap to close

Make product and business impact explicit: customer problem → business importance → product decision → design change → measurable outcome.

03 / Proof

Five executive stories are enough to start. Each must show your leadership decision and what changed because you were there.

01 · Product transformation

Choose a case where product experience, complexity or adoption changed.

02 · Systems transformation

Suite-first, design systems, cross-product harmonisation or sign-in/out.

03 · Organisation transformation

Central design, Design Hub India, capability, community or team growth.

04 · AI transformation

Joule, Collections Agent, Generative UI or another case where AI changed the product or design approach.

05 · Change leadership

A difficult stakeholder, ambiguity, conflict, turnaround or strategic shift where your leadership mattered.

07 / 90-day sprint

Three phases. The market starts in Week 1.

01

Days 1-14 · Position

  • Lock target role territory
  • Rewrite résumé + LinkedIn
  • Select five proof cases
  • Map 20-30 companies
  • Start warm outreach
  • Apply to first roles
02

Days 15-30 · Prove

  • Build executive case stories
  • Add metrics / outcomes
  • Build AI POV + case
  • Collect testimonials
  • Run mock interviews
  • Be interview-ready by Day 30
03

Days 31-90 · Convert

  • Maintain targeted pipeline
  • Refine stories from interviews
  • Publish one strong POV
  • Prepare executive conversations
  • Negotiate from evidence
  • Keep options open
Time allocation: 40% market · 30% evidence · 20% POV / product-business depth · 10% website.
The website is infrastructure. It is not the job search.

08 / Interview room

Prepare for the questions that determine whether your experience reads as senior leadership.

Leadership

How do you raise design quality across a large organisation? How do you coach leaders? How do you handle disagreement? How do you create accountability without slowing teams down?

Product + business

How did design change a business outcome? What metric mattered? What trade-off did you make? How do you know when a design decision is working?

AI

What changes in design when AI becomes part of the product? How do you design trust, control, uncertainty and human agency? Where should AI not be used?

Executive

Why are you moving now? What environment brings out your best work? What would you change in your first 90 days? What is the leadership problem you want to own next?
