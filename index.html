The resilience rule

The goal is not simply to get a job. The goal is to become significantly more valuable and more mobile in 90 days. A delayed offer changes the path, not the progress.

01 / Six sprints →

Every 15-day block has a clear exit condition and a fallback outcome.

02 / Market →

Applications, referrals, recruiters, conversations and pipeline.

03 / Articulation →

Build the ability to speak with executive clarity through repeated video practice.

04 / Proof →

Turn experience into evidence of product, systems, organisation and AI leadership.

05 / Readiness →

Track what must be ready and what capability gaps need closing.

06 / Resilience →

Know what happens if hiring, timing or external conditions do not cooperate.
Primary path: get the offer. Fallback path: build career optionality.
Never let one company's decision determine whether these 90 days were successful.
